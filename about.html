About us

General contracting for Abu Dhabi properties

We support building owners, property managers and businesses with technical services, renovations and maintenance.

Our company

Support for building projects and everyday repairs

Based in Abu Dhabi, Empowerment General Contracting LLC brings together building systems, civil works and interior finishing services.

Our scope includes A/C and ventilation, electrical and plumbing works, elevator maintenance, cleaning, tiling, interlock paving, ceilings and carpentry. Property managers can discuss recurring service needs, and owners can enquire about individual repairs or renovations involving several trades.

Quality, safety, integrity and on-time delivery guide our discussions about site conditions, work schedules and handover.
