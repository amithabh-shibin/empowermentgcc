Frequently asked questions

Planning work at your property?

Here are answers to common questions about services, quotations and site visits.

Which services can I request?

We take enquiries for MEP, A/C, ductwork, electrical and plumbing, cleaning, elevators, civil work, tiling, interlock paving, ceilings and carpentry.

Do you work on commercial and residential properties?

We welcome enquiries for commercial, residential and industrial properties in Abu Dhabi. Share your property type and location so the team can discuss your requirements.

What should I send for a quotation?

Include the location, the work you need, your preferred timeline and any photos or drawings you can share. The team can advise if a site visit is needed.

Can I request more than one trade for the same project?

Yes. Tell us about all the work involved so the team can discuss trade coordination, site access and the order in which work should happen.

Can you help with recurring maintenance?

You can enquire about scheduled upkeep for your property. Include the systems involved and how often you expect them to be serviced.
